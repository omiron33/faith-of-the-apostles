# Visual notes

The Faith of the Apostles follows the image of faith carried through darkness: flame passed from candle to candle, roads traveled, churches enduring and light reaching the empty tomb.

The choruses, Trisagion and Theotokos verse use a reconstruction of the Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood, with procedural mosaic and marble surfaces, candlelight and chandeliers. A darker Gothic nave carries the scenes of struggle. Verse worlds include the Sea of Galilee, the Apostles' journeys, a ruined church, the two shores of the schism, the empty tomb and glowing iron as a metaphor for theosis.

The v2 filioque sequence presents the disputed addition as a carved word that breaks. The surrounding imagery reflects the song's Orthodox theological perspective. This replaces the neon treatment from the first version.

Typography follows the sung onset of each word. Amber marks the central confession, pale capitals emphasize divine names, cold tones accompany division and softer italic voices carry mercy. The v2 revision also brightens candlelit interiors, varies repeated church shots and keeps the text within the frame.
